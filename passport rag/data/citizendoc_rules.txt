# SECTION 1: PASSPORT SEVA - PROOF OF ADDRESS & COMMON REJECTION TRAPS
Authority: Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India
Service Category: Passport Services (Normal & Tatkaal)
Rejection Risk Tier: HIGH / COUNTER REJECTION RISK

### 1.1 Acceptable Documents for Proof of Present Address (Mandatory Checklist)
The applicant must submit at least ONE of the following valid documents reflecting their CURRENT residing address:
1. Aadhaar Card (e-Aadhaar is acceptable provided QR code is verifiable).
2. Current passbook of running account with a Scheduled Public Sector Bank, Scheduled Private Sector Bank, or Regional Rural Bank (Must contain applicant's photograph stamped and signed across by the Branch Manager).
3. Water bill (Issued within the last 2 months; must be in applicant's or spouse's/parent's name).
4. Electricity bill (Issued within the last 2 months; must be in applicant's or spouse's/parent's name).
5. Landline or Postpaid Mobile phone bill (Issued within the last 2 months).
6. Election Photo Identity Card (EPIC / Voter ID).
7. Registered Rent Agreement (Strict Requirement: Must be registered under Section 17 of the Registration Act, 1908 with the Sub-Registrar. A simple Rs. 100 or Rs. 500 NOTARIZED rent agreement is strictly REJECTED at the PSK counter).
8. Gas Connection Bill or Passbook.
9. Certificate from Employer on official letterhead (Applicable only to reputed public/private limited companies).
10. Spouse's Passport copy (First and last page mentioning applicant's name as spouse, provided present address matches).

### 1.2 Top 4 Counter Rejection Pitfalls for Address Proof:
- TRAP #1 (Notarized Rent Agreement): Over 35% of tenant passport applications are rejected because applicants bring a notary-stamped rent agreement. MEA guidelines strictly mandate a Registered Rent Agreement registered at the Sub-Registrar's Office.
- TRAP #2 (Cooperative Bank Passbooks): Passbooks from urban cooperative banks, local credit societies, or private non-scheduled banks are NOT accepted. Only Scheduled Commercial Banks (e.g., SBI, HDFC, ICICI, PNB, Bank of Baroda) with branch seal across the photograph are valid.
- TRAP #3 (Address Mismatch with Utility Bills): Submitting an electricity or water bill older than 2 billing cycles leads to instant token cancellation at the document screening counter.
- TRAP #4 (Parents' Name Address for Adult Married Applicants): Adult married applicants submitting utility bills in their father's or mother's name without a marriage certificate or spouse passport linkage face documentation delays.

# SECTION 2: PROOF OF DATE OF BIRTH (DOB) & NON-ECR (EMIGRATION CHECK) RULES
Authority: Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India
Service Category: Fresh Passport / Minor to Adult Conversion
Rejection Risk Tier: CRITICAL / ELIGIBILITY DETERMINATION

### 2.1 Proof of Date of Birth (DOB) Modern Regulatory Reforms:
Under the revised Passport Rules (notified December 2016), the archaic rule mandating a Municipal Birth Certificate for applicants born on or after 26/01/1989 has been relaxed. Any ONE of the following 8 documents is legally acceptable as conclusive proof of DOB:
1. Birth Certificate issued by the Registrar of Births and Deaths or the Municipal Corporation / local body.
2. Transfer / School Leaving / Matriculation Certificate issued by a recognized educational board containing the date of birth.
3. PAN Card issued by the Income Tax Department with Date of Birth.
4. Aadhaar Card / e-Aadhaar mentioning date, month, and year of birth.
5. Driving License issued by the State Transport Department.
6. Election Photo Identity Card (Voter ID) containing Date of Birth.
7. Policy Bond issued by Public Life Insurance Corporations (LIC).
8. Service record / Pension payment order for government employees.

### 2.2 Non-ECR (Emigration Check Not Required) Status Rules:
An ECR passport prevents Indian citizens from traveling to 18 notified countries (including UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, Malaysia) for employment without clearance from the Protector of Emigrants (POE).
To obtain NON-ECR (formerly Emigration Check Not Required) status, applicants must submit:
1. Matriculation / 10th Standard Pass Certificate or higher educational degree from a recognized institution (Highest rejection cause: Submitting provisional 10th mark sheets without the final pass certificate).
2. Income Tax Payers: Assessment orders for the last 1 year and proof of payment of income tax.
3. Persons aged above 50 years (Verified via proof of DOB).
4. Children below 18 years of age.
5. Spouses of Non-ECR category passport holders (Must produce spouse's passport with endorsement).

# SECTION 3: TATKAAL PASSPORT APPLICATION - MANDATORY 3-DOCUMENT MATRIX
Authority: Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India
Service Category: Tatkaal Scheme Expedited Issuance
Rejection Risk Tier: CRITICAL / IMMEDIATE REJECTION AT PSK

### 3.1 Tatkaal Eligibility Criteria & Document Requirement:
Under the Tatkaal scheme, passports are dispatched within 1 to 3 working days without waiting for prior police verification. To qualify, adult applicants MUST submit at least THREE (3) documents from the following designated list of 13 documents:
1. Aadhaar Card / e-Aadhaar (Mandatory baseline document in almost all Tatkaal cases).
2. Electors Photo Identity Card (EPIC / Voter ID).
3. Permanent Account Number (PAN Card).
4. Driving License (Active status).
5. Bank / Kisan / Post Office Passbook with photo (Scheduled banks only).
6. Student Photo Identity card issued by a recognized educational institution.
7. Birth Certificate issued under the RBD Act.
8. Service Photo Identity Card issued by State/Central Government, PSU, or Public Limited company.
9. Arms License.
10. Pension Document or Ex-servicemen Identity Card.
11. SC / ST / OBC Certificate issued by competent authority.
12. Ration Card with photograph.
13. Freedom Fighter Identity Card.

### 3.2 Critical Tatkaal Rules & Common Pitfalls:
- Exact Name and Parent Name Consistency: All 3 selected documents MUST have exact, 100% character-by-character identical name, surname, and father's/mother's name spellings. A single letter variation (e.g., "Swaroop" vs "Swarup" or expanded initials "K. Kumar" vs "Kiran Kumar") results in automatic conversion from Tatkaal to Normal processing.
- Present Address Match: At least one of the 3 documents MUST explicitly mention the applicant's current residential address where physical police verification will subsequently occur.
- Standard Affidavit (Annexure E): Self-declaration stating citizenship, absence of criminal proceedings, and urgency justification must be submitted. Verification Certificate by Joint Secretary (Annexure F) is NO LONGER mandatory for regular citizens.

# SECTION 4: PASSPORT REISSUE - NAME CHANGE, CORRECTIONS & LOST PASSPORT
Authority: Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), Government of India
Service Category: Passport Reissue / Modification
Rejection Risk Tier: HIGH / LEGAL COMPLIANCE

### 4.1 Minor Name Spelling Correction vs Major Name Change:
1. Minor Spelling Correction (e.g., "Ramesh" to "Ramesh Kumar" or correcting typographical vowel errors):
   - Requires previous passport original.
   - Any 2 educational or government identity documents showing the correct spelling (e.g., PAN Card and 10th certificate).
   - No newspaper advertisement or gazette notification required.
2. Major Name Change (e.g., changing given name from "Amit" to "Rahul" or complete surname alteration):
   - Mandatory Requirement 1: Two (2) original newspaper advertisements clippings (one in a local vernacular daily, one in a national English daily) published in the area of permanent/present residence announcing the name change.
   - Mandatory Requirement 2: Original Gazette Notification published in the official Gazette of India or respective State Government Gazette.
   - Applications without the Gazette notification will NOT be entertained under any circumstances.

### 4.2 Name Change Post-Marriage (Wife Adopting Husband's Surname):
- Modern Reform: Marriage Certificate is NO LONGER mandatory for name change post-marriage.
- Submission of standard Annexure 'D' self-declaration along with self-attested copy of spouse's passport is sufficient.

### 4.3 Lost or Damaged Passport Procedure:
1. Lost Passport:
   - Mandatory: Police Report / Non-Traceable Certificate (NCR/FIR) from the local police station explicitly mentioning the lost passport number.
   - Duly executed Affidavit (Annexure 'F') explaining the circumstances of loss.
   - Self-attested copies of first 2 and last 2 pages of the lost passport (if available).
2. Damaged Passport:
   - If passport number, photograph, and applicant details are legible: Can be processed under normal renewal/reissue.
   - If passport is completely mutilated, burned, or page torn off: Treated as Lost Passport; requires Annexure 'F' and cannot be processed under Tatkaal.

# SECTION 5: DRIVING LICENSE (DL) & LEARNER LICENSE (LL) RULES UNDER PARIVAHAN
Authority: Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH), Government of India
Service Category: Parivahan Sarathi - Driving License Services
Rejection Risk Tier: HIGH / STATUTORY TIME LIMITATION

### 5.1 Learner's License to Permanent Driving License Conversion:
1. Mandatory Waiting Period: Applicant must hold an active Learner's License for a minimum of 30 days before booking a permanent DL driving test slot.
2. Statutory Expiry: Learner's License is valid for exactly 180 days (6 months) from the date of issue.
3. Common Rejection Pitfall: Attempting to book a permanent DL slot before the 30-day window or after 180 days. If expired past 180 days, the applicant must re-apply for a fresh Learner's License from scratch.
4. Mandatory Documents for Permanent DL Test:
   - Original Learner's License (Form 3).
   - Form 2 application submission receipt.
   - Proof of vehicle matching the category applied for (Motorcycle with Gear - MCWG, Light Motor Vehicle - LMV) with valid Registration Certificate (RC), valid insurance, and Pollution Under Control (PUC) certificate.

### 5.2 Driving License Renewal & The 1-Year Expiry Re-Test Rule:
Under the amended Section 15 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 2019:
1. Renewal Window: DL can be renewed up to 1 year prior to the expiry date and up to 1 year after the expiry date.
2. The Critical 1-Year Expiry Rule (Severe Trap): If a driving license has expired for more than one (1) year, the applicant MUST undergo the formal practical driving test again at the RTO test track before renewal is granted.
3. Form 1A Medical Certificate:
   - Mandatory for all commercial transport vehicle drivers.
   - Mandatory for non-transport private drivers aged above 40 years.
   - Must be issued and digitally uploaded by a registered medical practitioner (MBBS).

# SECTION 6: DIGILOCKER & mPARIVAHAN LEGAL VALIDITY (RULE 139 CMVR)
Authority: Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH), Government of India
Service Category: Enforcement, Traffic Police & RTO Inspection
Rejection Risk Tier: CRITICAL / CITIZEN RIGHTS & POLICE ENFORCEMENT

### 6.1 Legal Status of Digital Driving License and RC (Rule 139 of CMVR, 1989):
Under Rule 139 of the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989 and MoRTH Official Advisory Notification No. RT-11036/64/2017-MVL:
1. Physical Original Exemption: Production of Driving License (DL), Registration Certificate (RC), Fitness Certificate, Insurance, and PUC in electronic form on DigiLocker or mParivahan official mobile apps is 100% LEGALLY VALID across all states and Union Territories of India.
2. Police / RTO Officer Cannot Insist on Physical Originals: Traffic police officers, transport department inspectors, or RTO counters CANNOT demand physical cards or issue challans under Section 130/177 of the Motor Vehicles Act if valid electronic credentials are shown.
3. Screenshots or WhatsApp PDFs are NOT Accepted: The digital document must be pulled directly from the authenticated DigiLocker or mParivahan app with the verifiable digital signature / QR code. Stored camera photos or scanned WhatsApp PDFs do not hold legal validity.

### 6.2 Electronic Impounding of Documents:
If an offense warrants seizure of the Driving License (e.g., drunk driving, dangerous driving, jumping red light under Section 206(4)):
- Traffic police or enforcement officers execute electronic seizure via the e-Challan national database.
- Physical confiscation of the card is NOT required if the license is electronically marked as impounded on the Sarathi database.

# SECTION 7: RTO VEHICLE SERVICES - INTERSTATE TRANSFER & NOC (FORM 28)
Authority: Ministry of Road Transport and Highways (MoRTH), Government of India
Service Category: Parivahan Vahan - Vehicle Ownership & Relocation
Rejection Risk Tier: HIGH / INTERSTATE COMPLIANCE

### 7.1 No Objection Certificate (NOC - Form 28) for Interstate Relocation:
When moving a registered motor vehicle from one State/UT to another for a period exceeding 12 months (under Section 47 of the Motor Vehicles Act):
1. Application Requirements:
   - Form 28 in triplicate (3 copies) with clear pencil print of the vehicle chassis number on all copies (Highest rejection reason: Smudged, illegible, or missing pencil imprint of the chassis number).
   - Certified copy of the Registration Certificate (RC).
   - Valid Certificate of Insurance.
   - Valid Pollution Under Control (PUC) Certificate.
   - Commercial vehicles: Clearance report from local police crime record bureau (NCRB) verifying no pending theft or accident cases.
2. Validity of NOC: An issued NOC does not expire; however, the vehicle must be re-registered in the destination state within a reasonable time.
3. State Road Tax Refund: After paying lifetime road tax in the new state, the owner can apply for a pro-rata road tax refund from the original registering RTO by submitting the new RC copy and Form 28 confirmation.

### 7.2 Transfer of Ownership (Sale of Vehicle):
1. Within Same State: Form 29 (Notice of Transfer) + Form 30 (Report of Transfer) submitted within 14 days of vehicle sale.
2. Interstate Buyer: NOC (Form 28) mandatory from original RTO, followed by Form 29, Form 30, and re-registration (Form 20) in the buyer's state within 45 days.
